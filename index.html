Started in a garage. Still run that way.

Ember & Oak opened in 2019 with one roaster, two farms, and a folding table for a counter. Six years later the roaster's bigger, but the approach hasn't changed — small batches, direct relationships with growers, and nothing on the shelf longer than three weeks.

Every bag lists the farm, the elevation, and the date it was roasted, because you should know exactly what you're drinking and who grew it.
